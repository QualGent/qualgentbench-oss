## Users
- [users-001] Users track personal finances by hand: they record each withdrawal, deposit and transfer across their own bank, cash and savings accounts. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## What matters to them
- [matters-001] Correct numbers matter most, because users rely on the app to know how much money they have. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [matters-002] Users own their data: it is kept in a local database file with no required online account. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Terminology
- [term-001] A "transaction" is a withdrawal (money out), a deposit (money in) or a transfer (money moved between two of the user's accounts). <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=source -->
- [term-002] A "payee" is who the money goes to or comes from, and each payee can carry a default "category" such as Food. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=source -->
- [term-003] Each transaction has a "status": None, Reconciled, Void, Follow-up or Duplicate. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=source -->
