# Org memory: Orgzly Revived

## Company
- [company-001] Orgzly Revived is a community-maintained continuation of the Orgzly app. The community took it over when the original author disappeared and its development stopped. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [company-002] The app is open source under the GNU GPL and ships on F-Droid, IzzyOnDroid and Google Play. It is made by volunteers, with support through GitHub Discussions and a Matrix room. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Users
- [users-001] Users take notes and manage to-do lists on Android, often alongside Emacs Org mode on a computer. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## What matters to them
- [matters-001] Notebooks are plain-text Org mode files, so users keep full control of their data and can open it in other tools. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [matters-002] Sync is optional and user-chosen: a folder on the device or SD card, a WebDAV server, or Dropbox. Compatibility with Org mode's format and rules is a core value. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Terminology
- [term-001] A "notebook" is one Org file. A "note" is a heading in it, and notes nest under other notes to form an outline. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [term-002] A note can have a state (such as TODO or DONE), a priority letter, tags, a "Scheduled" time and a "Deadline" time. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=source -->
- [term-003] A "repeater" on a timestamp makes a task recur. "Repositories" are the sync locations. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=source -->
