# Org memory: Alinson S. Xavier (iSoron)

## Company
- [company-001] Loop Habit Tracker is an open-source habit tracking app started by Alinson S. Xavier (iSoron) and developed by volunteers. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [company-002] The app is licensed GPL-3.0 and is distributed on Google Play and F-Droid, with APKs on GitHub releases; it has no in-app purchases. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Users
- [users-001] Users build and keep good habits, checking off each day and following their progress in graphs and statistics. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [users-002] Many users rely on reminders and home screen widgets to check habits without opening the app. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## What matters to them
- [matters-001] The app works offline with no account, and habit data is never sent to anyone. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [matters-002] The app is ad-free, with no limit on the number of habits and every feature available to every user. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [matters-003] Users own their data and can export it to CSV or an SQLite database file. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Terminology
- [term-001] The habit "score" (habit strength) grows with each repetition and drops slowly with missed days, so a few misses do not erase progress. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [term-002] A habit is either "Yes or No" (done or not each day) or "Measurable" (a number with a unit and a target). <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=source -->
