# Org memory: Tasks.org (Alex Baker)

## Company
- [company-001] Tasks.org is an open-source to-do app for Android, developed mainly by Alex Baker in the public GitHub project tasks/tasks. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [company-002] The app is based on the source code of Astrid, an open-source task app that was discontinued in 2013. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [company-003] It is distributed on Google Play and F-Droid, and the project accepts donations through PayPal, Liberapay and Bitcoin. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Users
- [users-001] Users are people who manage personal to-do lists, many of whom moved from Astrid, and who rely on due dates, reminders and repeating tasks. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [users-002] Some users keep tasks only on the device, and others sync them with services such as Google Tasks or a CalDAV server. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## What matters to them
- [matters-001] Reliability of scheduling matters most: due dates, repeats and reminders are the reason people use the app. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [matters-002] The project values open source and user control of data, with no account needed to keep tasks on the device. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Terminology
- [term-001] A "list" holds tasks, and a "filter" is a saved view that collects tasks across lists, such as tasks due today. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [term-002] A "subtask" is a task filed under a parent task, and a task's "priority" is shown as the color of its completion circle. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
