# Org memory: Fossify

## Company
- [company-001] Fossify is a community organization (FossifyOrg on GitHub) that maintains a suite of simple Android apps forked from Simple Mobile Tools in 2023. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [company-002] The apps are open source under GPLv3, free, and distributed on F-Droid, Google Play and GitHub releases; the project is funded by donations. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Users
- [users-001] Users are mostly privacy-minded Android users who replace the stock phone apps (calendar, contacts, files, gallery) with Fossify ones. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## What matters to them
- [matters-001] The apps have no ads, no tracking and no account; they work offline and keep data on the device. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [matters-002] Users expect their own data (events, contacts, files, photos) to stay exactly as they saved it. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Terminology
- [term-001] All apps share one library (Fossify Commons), so dialogs, the Settings screen, color customization and the toolbar look the same across the suite. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=source -->
- [term-002] "More options" is the three-dot overflow menu; long-pressing an item starts a selection with its own action bar and More options menu. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=source -->
