# Org memory: Anton Razinkov (Razeeman)

## Company
- [company-001] Simple Time Tracker is an open-source time tracking app developed by Anton Razinkov, who publishes on GitHub as Razeeman. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [company-002] The Android app is licensed GPL-3.0 and is distributed on F-Droid, Google Play and GitHub releases; the project takes donations through Buy Me a Coffee. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Users
- [users-001] Users track how much time they spend on everyday activities, starting and stopping timers with one tap and reviewing records and statistics later. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [users-002] Some users also track from home screen widgets, notifications or a Wear OS watch (Play version only). <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## What matters to them
- [matters-001] The app works offline with no account; tracked data never leaves the phone. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [matters-002] There are no ads, in-app purchases or intrusive permissions. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [matters-003] A minimal interface that is quick to use, and records that can be backed up or exported to CSV. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Terminology
- [term-001] An "activity" is a thing the user tracks; a running "timer" becomes a "record" when it is stopped. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=cases -->
- [term-002] "Categories" group activities, and "record tags" label individual records. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=cases -->
