# Org memory: AnkiDroid open source team

## Company
- [company-001] AnkiDroid is the official Android port of Anki, the open-source spaced repetition flashcard system, built by a volunteer open-source team on GitHub (ankidroid/Anki-Android). <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [company-002] The app is licensed under GPL-3.0 and distributed free on Google Play and F-Droid, with funding through donations on Open Collective. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [company-003] Translation is community-run on Crowdin, and the app is available in close to 100 languages. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Users
- [users-001] Users are learners who study every day, such as language learners and medical students, and many also use Anki on a computer and sync through AnkiWeb. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## What matters to them
- [matters-001] A user's collection holds years of study history, so losing notes, cards or review history is the worst outcome. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [matters-002] Compatibility with Anki desktop and AnkiWeb matters: the same collection and scheduling must work across all of them. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Terminology
- [term-001] A "note" holds the fields a user types (a Basic note has "Front" and "Back"), and a "card" is one question and answer generated from a note. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [term-002] A "deck" groups cards for study, and the "collection" is the whole set of decks, notes, cards and review history. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [term-003] During review the user grades recall with "Again", "Hard", "Good" or "Easy", and the scheduler picks when the card is due next. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
