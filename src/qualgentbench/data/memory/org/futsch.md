## Users
- [users-001] Users are people who take medicines on a schedule and want reminders, a record of what they took, and a warning before a medicine runs out. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## What matters to them
- [matters-001] All data stays on the phone and the app needs no internet connection. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [matters-002] An accurate dose history matters to users, because they use it to show adherence and to share it as an export. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Terminology
- [term-001] A "medicine" has one or more "reminders". Each reminder has a time or interval and a dosage. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [term-002] An "event" is one dose on the Overview. It is raised by a reminder and answered as "Taken" or "Skipped", or rescheduled. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=cases -->
- [term-003] "Stock" is the amount of a medicine on hand. The app can warn when stock runs low. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
