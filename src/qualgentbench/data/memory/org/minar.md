# Org memory: minar (Birday)

## Company
- [company-001] Birday is made by an independent developer who publishes as minar (GitHub m-i-n-a-r). It began as a free-time project and was first published on May 1, 2020. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [company-002] The app is open source under GPL 3 and is free on Google Play and F-Droid. Translations come from volunteers through Crowdin. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Users
- [users-001] Users want to remember birthdays and other yearly events without Facebook, Google Calendar or manual alarms, including people who are not in their contacts. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## What matters to them
- [matters-001] The stated focus is an app that is easy to use, clean and lightweight. New features are added only when they are really useful. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [matters-002] Users rely on a notification on the day of each event, and they can back up and restore their saved events. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Terminology
- [term-001] An "event" is one entry. Its type is Birthday, Anniversary, Death anniversary, Name day or Other. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [term-002] "Favorites" are starred events, shown on their own screen with more detail and a countdown. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [term-003] "Consider the year" is the switch that decides if an event's date has a known year. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=source -->
