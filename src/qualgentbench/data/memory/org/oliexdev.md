# Org memory: olie.xdev (openScale)

## Company
- [company-001] openScale is an open-source weight and body metrics tracker made by olie.xdev (GitHub oliexdev), a solo developer, with help from community contributors. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [company-002] The app is licensed under GPL v3, distributed on F-Droid and as an open beta on Google Play, and funded by donations through PayPal and GitHub Sponsors. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Users
- [users-001] Users track their weight and body composition over time, often with a Bluetooth scale from brands such as Beurer, Sanitas, Yunmai or Xiaomi, and some enter measurements by hand. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [users-002] One device can hold several users, such as family members, each with their own profile and measurements. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## What matters to them
- [matters-001] Privacy matters most: the app needs no account, has no ads and sends no data to a cloud. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [matters-002] Users rely on long measurement histories, so losing data is the worst outcome. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Terminology
- [term-001] A "measurement" is one dated entry with a weight and other body values such as body fat, water and muscle. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [term-002] Some values, such as BMI, are computed by the app from the weight and the user's profile (height, birth date, sex). <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
