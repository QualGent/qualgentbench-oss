# Org memory: Catima

## Company
- [company-001] Catima is a community libre software project, published by the CatimaLoyalty organization on GitHub and led by Sylvia van Os (TheLastProject). <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [company-002] The app is copylefted libre software under GPLv3+. It ships on F-Droid, IzzyOnDroid, GitHub and Google Play. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [company-003] Catima is free of charge and is funded by community contributions through GitHub Sponsors and PayPal donations. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Users
- [users-001] Users replace plastic loyalty cards, coupons, gift cards and tickets with barcodes on their phone. Many move over from FidMe, Loyalty Card Keychain or Voucher Vault. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## What matters to them
- [matters-001] Privacy comes first: no internet access, no ads, very few permissions and no personal data collected. Crash reports are sent only when the user chooses to. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
- [matters-002] Users own their data through backups and import/export, and the app supports a dark theme and accessibility options for vision-impaired users. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->

## Terminology
- [term-001] A "card" has a "Name" (the store), a "Card ID", an optional barcode, a note and a balance. <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=source -->
- [term-002] Cards can be starred as favorites, archived, and sorted into "Groups". <!-- src=human; confirmed=2026-11-17; seen=0; contra=0; basis=readme -->
