# Code of Conduct

Be kind, be honest, be patient. That is most of it.

**We expect everyone here to:**

- treat others with respect, whatever their experience level, background, or
  identity;
- give feedback about the work, not the person — "this replay is wrong because…"
  is great, "you clearly don't understand…" is not;
- assume good intent, and say so when something reads badly — text loses tone;
- accept that maintainers sometimes say no, and that a no is not an insult.

**We won't accept:**

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, PRs, or anywhere else the
  project lives;
- publishing someone's private information;
- sustained disruption — derailing threads, reopening settled arguments, spam.

**If something happens:** report it privately to the maintainers. Reports are
read by a human, kept confidential, and acted on — anything from a quiet word to
a ban, depending on what happened.

This applies to everyone, maintainers included.
